## Label source

Labels were made by visually inspecting the original MP4, not by copying descriptions or time ranges from `pipeline.parse.json`. I generated temporary ffmpeg contact sheets at 0.5- to 5-second sampling intervals under `/private/tmp/seekino-ep07-recall` and inspected the relevant frames at original detail. The series bible was used only to normalize visually confirmed main-character aliases. VideoChunk numbers were then derived from the canonical 60-second boundaries.

## Interpretation limits

- This is a small smoke set for qualitative and early quantitative checks, not a statistically representative benchmark.
- Several queries intentionally share boundary-adjacent footage; aggregate metrics should therefore be read together with per-query rankings.
- No negative or distractor labels are included yet.
- Because every parsed VideoChunk has `is_fallback=false`, a separate fixture must be created after a real fallback VideoChunk exists.

## First real run: 2026-09-13

The smoke set was run against all 24 canonical, zero-overlap VideoChunks with the
configured Ark multimodal embedding endpoint at 1024 dimensions and 1 FPS. The
text channel embeds only `self_compact` plus owned events; it never reads
`window_summary` or a neighboring context snapshot.

| Channel | Hit@1 | Hit@3 | Hit@5 | Hit@10 | MRR |

This establishes that the current representation is useful as a raw
high-recall candidate generator: every query finds a relevant VideoChunk by Top
10. It is not reliable as a final ranker. Text is materially stronger than
vision on this set, and equal weighting makes the combined Top 1/3 and MRR worse
than text alone.

The hardest failure, `r06`, is directly connected to the parser audit: the
correct words are present in VideoChunk 10, but its descriptions attribute 乐奈's
answers to 高松灯. The entity contradiction pushes that VideoChunk down to rank 8
in both channels. Vision is valuable for the embrace, emotional outburst, and
phone shot, but a whole 60-second video vector is noisy for dialogue-heavy and
on-screen-title queries.

As a diagnostic only, re-scoring the same 12 labels with text:vision RRF weight
`3:1` produces Hit@1 75.0%, Hit@3 91.7%, Hit@5 91.7%, Hit@10 100%, and MRR
0.845. This is in-sample tuning, not a held-out result, so it must not become a
production default from this smoke set alone. The next defensible step is a
larger held-out fixture followed by a CandidateVerifier or a finer-grained
candidate stage.
