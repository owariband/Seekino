# Say a name. Get every second they appear.

This is what we want to do next: across a whole series, Seekino won't just find one scene. It will list every moment a character appears, with timestamps, without missing one.

## Planned

- **Every appearance of a character** — Name a character and get every part of the series they appear in.
- **Personal libraries on Cloud** — Sign in to upload and parse your own videos on Cloud, with nothing to deploy.
- **Reusable editing references** — Remember the camera moves and motion Seekino has parsed, so an agent can reuse them in the next edit.

None of these have dates yet. Which one do you want first? Tell us in the community group; one message from you can decide what we build next.
